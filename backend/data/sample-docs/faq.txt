Employee FAQ

How many annual leave days do employees receive?
Employees receive 21 working days of paid annual leave per year, accrued monthly.

How do I request time off?
Submit a leave request through the HR portal at least 5 business days before the requested dates. Your manager will approve or decline the request within 2 business days.

Can I work from home?
Yes. Employees may work remotely up to 3 days per week with manager approval. Fully remote setups require VP approval.

What is the process for submitting expenses?
Upload an itemized receipt to the finance portal within 30 days of the purchase. Expenses over $50 need manager approval before you spend the money.

Who do I contact for IT issues?
Email it-support@guidely.example or open a ticket in the IT helpdesk portal. Critical outages should also be reported in the #it-urgent Slack channel.

How do I reset my company password?
Go to the identity portal at id.guidely.example and click "Forgot password". You will receive a reset link at your registered company email within a few minutes. Passwords must be at least 12 characters and include a number and symbol.

What health benefits are offered?
The company provides medical, dental, and vision insurance starting on your first day of employment, with premiums 80% covered by the company for employees and 50% covered for dependents.

How is performance reviewed?
Formal performance reviews happen twice a year, in June and December. Reviews include a self-assessment, manager assessment, and peer feedback, and feed into compensation decisions.

What is the parental leave policy?
Primary caregivers receive 16 weeks of paid parental leave; secondary caregivers receive 6 weeks. Leave can be taken within 12 months of the birth or adoption and may be split into two blocks.

Who approves new software purchases?
Software purchases under $200/month can be approved by a team lead. Anything above that requires IT and Finance sign-off through the procurement portal.

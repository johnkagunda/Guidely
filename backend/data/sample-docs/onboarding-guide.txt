Employee Onboarding Guide

Before Your First Day
HR will send you an offer letter, a link to complete tax and banking paperwork, and instructions for your first day at least one week before your start date. Please complete all paperwork at least 2 business days before you start so your accounts can be provisioned in time.

Day One
Arrive at 9am and check in at reception, where you'll receive your access badge. Your manager will walk you through the org chart, introduce you to your team, and help you complete IT setup, including your laptop, email, and Slack account. HR will run a benefits enrollment session in the afternoon.

First Week Checklist
Complete mandatory compliance training, including data privacy and workplace conduct modules, within your first 5 working days. Meet with your assigned onboarding buddy at least twice during week one. Set up 1:1 meetings with your manager and key cross-functional partners.

30-60-90 Day Plan
By day 30, you should understand your team's goals and have completed initial training. By day 60, you should be contributing to real project work with guidance. By day 90, you should be operating with increasing independence, and your manager will conduct a formal 90-day check-in to discuss progress and set goals for the following quarter.

Benefits Enrollment
New employees must enroll in health, dental, and vision benefits within 30 days of their start date, or they will need to wait until the next open enrollment period unless a qualifying life event occurs. Retirement plan enrollment can happen at any time after your start date.

Mentorship Program
All new hires are paired with an onboarding buddy for their first 30 days and may optionally join the longer-term mentorship program, which pairs employees with a senior mentor outside their immediate team for career guidance.

Probation Period
New employees are subject to a 90-day probationary period, during which either party may end employment with 1 week's notice. Performance and fit are formally reviewed at the end of the probation period.

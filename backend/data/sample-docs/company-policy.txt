Company Policy Handbook

Annual Leave
Employees are entitled to 21 working days of paid annual leave per calendar year, accrued monthly at a rate of 1.75 days per month. Leave must be requested at least five business days in advance through the HR portal and approved by a direct manager. Unused leave of up to 5 days may be carried over into the following year; any balance beyond that is forfeited on December 31st unless local law requires otherwise. Part-time employees receive annual leave on a pro-rata basis according to their contracted hours.

Sick Leave
Employees receive 10 paid sick days per year. A doctor's note is required for absences longer than 3 consecutive days. Sick leave does not carry over to the next year and is not paid out upon termination.

Remote Work Policy
Employees may work remotely up to 3 days per week with manager approval. Fully remote arrangements require VP-level sign-off and are reviewed every 6 months. Remote employees must be reachable during core hours of 10am-4pm local time and must attend in-person meetings when requested with at least one week's notice.

Expense Reimbursement
Business expenses must be submitted within 30 days of purchase via the finance portal, accompanied by an itemized receipt. Expenses under $50 do not require pre-approval; expenses over $50 require manager sign-off before the purchase is made. Reimbursements are processed within 10 business days of approval. Personal expenses, alcohol at non-client events, and first-class travel are not reimbursable.

Code of Conduct
All employees are expected to treat colleagues, clients, and partners with respect and professionalism. Harassment, discrimination, and retaliation of any kind are strictly prohibited and may result in termination. Employees must disclose any conflicts of interest to their manager or HR as soon as they become aware of them.

Termination and Notice Period
Employees on permanent contracts must provide a minimum of 4 weeks' written notice before resigning. The company may terminate employment for cause immediately, or without cause with 4 weeks' notice or pay in lieu of notice, in accordance with local employment law. Final paychecks, including any accrued unused leave, are issued within 14 days of the last working day.

Employee Guide

Getting Started
Welcome to Guidely. Your first week focuses on setting up your accounts, meeting your team, and completing mandatory training. Your manager will assign you a buddy for your first 30 days who can answer day-to-day questions.

Office Locations and Hours
The Nairobi office is open Monday through Friday, 8am-6pm, with core collaboration hours of 10am-4pm. Badge access is required outside of these hours. The London and Austin offices follow similar core-hours models adjusted for local time zones.

Communication Tools
The company uses Slack for daily communication, Google Workspace for email and documents, and Zoom for video meetings. Status updates for cross-team projects should be posted in the relevant #project channel every Friday.

Equipment and IT
New employees receive a laptop, monitor, and headset within their first week. Equipment requests for additional hardware go through the IT portal and are typically fulfilled within 5 business days. All company laptops must have disk encryption and the endpoint security agent installed before being used to access company systems.

Career Development
Employees can access a $1,500 annual learning and development stipend for courses, conferences, or certifications relevant to their role. Internal mobility is encouraged; employees who have been in their current role for at least 12 months may apply for internal job postings without needing manager pre-approval to apply.

Team Meetings and Cadence
Most teams run a weekly planning meeting on Mondays and a retrospective every other Friday. All-hands company meetings are held on the first Wednesday of each month and are recorded for anyone unable to attend live.

Travel Policy
Business travel must be booked through the corporate travel portal. Economy class is standard for flights under 6 hours; premium economy is permitted for longer flights. Travel insurance is automatically included for all company-booked trips.
